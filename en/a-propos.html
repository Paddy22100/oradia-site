Who am I?

Exploring what I live through, recognizing what nourishes me, and clarifying what calls to me.

I am a facilitator and guide in personal and collective transformation, and the creator of the oracle The Inner Compass.

I currently practice as a magnetic healer, after spending several years supporting people as a naturopath. This path around the body, energy, and listening deeply shapes the way I accompany others.

I support individuals and groups who want to step back from what they're living through, better understand their experiences, and let more aligned choices emerge. In a world that often moves very fast, it can be precious to pause for a moment, put words on what's happening, and look at a situation from another angle.

"My role isn't to give ready-made answers, but to create a space for listening, dialogue, and reflection that lets awareness and new perspectives emerge."

An Approach Rooted in Lived Experience

The Inner Compass

The Inner Compass is a guidance oracle I designed to support these explorations.

Drawing on both the dynamics of human experience and older symbolic structures, it sheds light on what you're living through and opens up new perspectives of understanding.

The Inner Compass is not a predictive tool.

It is, above all, a support for dialogue, reflection, and awareness.

Why The Inner Compass is different

A quantum draw, not a random one

Unlike traditional oracles based on pure chance, The Inner Compass uses a quantum approach that recognizes the deep interconnection between your consciousness and the draw. The cards aren't dealt "at random" — they respond to your state of consciousness in the moment.

Inspired by the science of consciousness

How The Inner Compass works draws on the research of physicist Philippe Guillemant on the quantum nature of consciousness and time. This research shows that our consciousness actively influences reality, making each draw deeply personal and revealing.

A conversation with your inner wisdom

Where other oracles give you answers from the outside, The Inner Compass puts you in dialogue with your own inner wisdom. Each draw reveals what you already know deep down, but which is seeking to emerge into your awareness.

"The Inner Compass doesn't tell you what will happen,
it reveals what's seeking to be born in you."

My Intention

Through these sessions and the creation of The Inner Compass, my intention is simple: to help create spaces where lived experience can become awareness, and a path toward evolution.

Whether personal or within a group, these moments of stepping back often bring more clarity, coherence, and freedom to the choices you make.
