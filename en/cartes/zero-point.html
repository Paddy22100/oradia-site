Zero Point Card

« At the center, everything hangs suspended in a dense silence. There, the birth of a new world takes shape. »

The Zero Point card, Cosmic Memory family, evokes a center where everything hangs suspended in a dense silence, where the birth of a new world takes shape. It signals a turning point, a starting point rather than an ending. Drawing this card invites you to recognize this beginning, even if it hasn't yet taken visible form.

This card belongs to the ten Cosmic Memory cards, a family with no mirror card, which brings a wider perspective (time, memory, cycles) when the draw calls for it.

This page gives the card's general meaning. In a real draw, the Zero Point card is always read in relation to the other cards drawn and the intention set at the start — it's that combined reading, unique to each draw, that the online oracle develops.
