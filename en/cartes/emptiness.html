Emptiness Card

« The empty cup waits to be filled. »

The Emptiness card, Transmutation family, yin polarity, evokes an empty cup waiting to be filled. It signals a space not yet occupied, rather than a permanent lack. Drawing this card invites you to welcome this vacancy without rushing to fill it.

In Oradia's mirror-card system, Emptiness answers to Fullness: the two don't oppose each other, they answer each other, like two sides of the same movement.

This page gives the card's general meaning. In a real draw, the Emptiness card is always read in relation to the other cards drawn and the intention set at the start — it's that combined reading, unique to each draw, that the online oracle develops.
