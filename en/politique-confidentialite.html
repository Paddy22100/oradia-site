Introduction

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and French data protection law, ORADIA is committed to protecting the privacy of its users and ensuring the security of their personal data.

This privacy policy describes how I collect, use, protect, and share your personal information when you use the ORADIA site.

  • First name

  • Postal address
  • Postal code
  • City

Payment data

  • Payment information processed by Stripe
  • Transaction history
  • Subscription information (where applicable)

Profile and usage data

  • Profile information (photo, personal description)
  • Card draw history
  • Personal notes and intentions
  • Account preferences and settings

Video guidance data

  • Name and email transmitted by Cal.com when booking
  • Time slot and duration of the session
  • Content of the draw performed during the session (cards drawn, intention set, AI analysis)
  • Jitsi Meet link for the session

Anonymous draw intentions

  • Intention set during a draw without an account (non-logged-in visitors)
  • Associated cards drawn
  • No identifying data is collected for these entries

Technical and statistical data

  • IP address (anonymized)

  • Pages visited and browsing time
  • Cookies and similar technologies

Legal basis for processing

Processing of your data relies on the following legal bases:

Consent

For optional data (newsletter, user reviews, etc.)

Contract performance

For data necessary to provide the services requested

Legal obligations

For data required by law (invoicing, archiving)

Legitimate interests

For service improvement and data security

Data retention period

Data is retained for as long as necessary to manage orders and meet legal obligations.

User account data

Kept for the entire duration of service use, then deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request.

Order and delivery data

Kept for 5 years for order management, invoicing, and accounting obligations.

Payment data

Bank details are not stored. Only transaction information is kept for 5 years for accounting purposes.

Draw history

Kept for 3 years after the last draw, unless earlier deletion is requested.

Newsletter

Data kept until unsubscribed. History kept for 6 months for traceability.

Technical data

Anonymized logs and statistics kept for a maximum of 13 months.

Data security

ORADIA implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all communications
  • Secure hosting on GDPR-compliant servers
  • Strict access control to data
  • Regular, secure backups
  • Staff training on data protection
  • Security incident management procedures

Cookies and similar technologies

No advertising tracker

Oradia uses no Google Analytics, no Tag Manager, and no third-party advertising pixel (Facebook, TikTok, etc.). The site's audience measurement is built and hosted in-house, with no tracking cookie set and no browsing data shared with an advertising third party.

This is unusual for a website, and it feels right to us: it keeps the site's technical footprint lighter (fewer third-party scripts loaded on every visit), simplifies your privacy (no advertising profiling is possible), and simplifies our legal compliance (nothing to ask consent for, since no advertising tracking exists in the first place).

Essential cookies

Necessary for the site to function (authentication, security, preferences).

In-house audience measurement

To understand how the site is used (pages visited, where visitors come from), we only use technical session identifiers stored locally in your browser (sessionStorage) — never a cross-site tracking cookie or a third-party service.

Functionality cookies

To remember your preferences and personalize your experience.

You can configure your browser to refuse cookies or delete existing ones.

Your GDPR rights

Right of access

You can request a copy of all your personal data.

Right to rectification

You can correct inaccurate information about you.

Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")

You can request the deletion of your personal data.

Right to data portability

You can retrieve your data in a structured format.

Right to object

You can object to the processing of your data for legitimate reasons.

Right to restriction

You can request that processing of your data be restricted.

How to exercise your rights

To exercise your GDPR rights, you can:

By email

Send your request to: contact@oradia.fr

Information to provide

  • First and last name
  • Email address of your ORADIA account
  • Right being exercised
  • Details of your request

Response time

I commit to responding within one month at the latest.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from your member area.

Deletion results in:

  • Immediate deletion of your profile
  • Anonymization of your draw history
  • Deletion of your contact data
  • Immediate stop of the newsletter

Some data may be retained for legal obligations (invoicing, archiving).

Subcontractors and transfers

The following subcontractors are involved in processing your data:

Vercel Inc. (United States) — Website and serverless function hosting.

Supabase Inc. (United States) — Encrypted database (profiles, draws, orders, guidance sessions).

Brevo SAS (France / European Union) — Transactional and newsletter email delivery.

Stripe Inc. (United States) — Payment processing. No bank data is stored by Oradia.

Cal.com Inc. (United States) — Booking of video guidance sessions. Data transmitted: name, email, time slot.

Anthropic PBC (United States) — AI analysis of draws and intentions (Claude API). Intentions and cards are transmitted to generate the analysis; they are not used to train the model.

8x8 Inc. / Jitsi Meet (United States) — Video conferencing for guidance sessions. Audio and video streams pass through their servers for the duration of the session.

Transfers to subcontractors located outside the European Union are governed by appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses or a European Commission adequacy decision).
